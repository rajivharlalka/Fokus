# ⚡ Quick Start - Deploy Your Flight Tracker Now!

## 🚀 Deploy to Vercel (2 Minutes - FREE)

### Step 1: Click This Link

On your phone or any device, open:

**https://vercel.com/new/clone?repository-url=https://github.com/rajivharlalka/Fokus/tree/cursor/web-version-d737**

### Step 2: Sign in with GitHub

- Click "Continue with GitHub"
- Authorize Vercel

### Step 3: Deploy

- Click "Deploy"
- Wait 2 minutes
- **Done!** Your app is live

### Step 4: Use Your App

You'll get a URL like: `https://your-app.vercel.app`

- Open it on your phone
- Search for flights: **AA100**, **DL200**, **UA300**
- Track flights
- Add to home screen (works like an app!)

## 📱 Add to Home Screen

### iPhone (Safari)

1. Open your Vercel app URL
2. Tap Share button (square with arrow)
3. Scroll down, tap "Add to Home Screen"
4. Tap "Add"
5. App icon appears on home screen!

### Android (Chrome)

1. Open your Vercel app URL
2. Tap menu (3 dots)
3. Tap "Add to Home screen"
4. Tap "Add"
5. App icon appears!
